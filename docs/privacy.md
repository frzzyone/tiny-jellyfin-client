# Tinyfin privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026. Applies to Tinyfin for iPhone, Apple TV and Mac.

**Tinyfin collects no data.** There are no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no
accounts or servers run by Tinyfin's developer. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer
or to anyone else.

The app talks only to the Jellyfin server you sign in to. To browse and play your library it sends
that server what any Jellyfin client does: your sign-in, what you play and how far you got, so the
server can resume and mark things watched. That server and what it records belong to you or to
whoever runs it; its own privacy terms apply.

On your device:

- Your sign-in token is kept in the device's Keychain and is not synced through iCloud.
- The server's address and a few view preferences (such as whether the iPhone player fills the
  screen) stay on the device.
- Films and episodes you download on iPhone stay in the app's storage until you or the app delete
  them, and go away when the app is deleted.
- Local network access is used only to reach a server on your home network.

Tinyfin is an independent app, not an official Jellyfin product.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/frzzyone/tiny-jellyfin-client/issues).
